Essential SEO for every new business website

Ahmad AlawiehJanuary 20264 min read

You do not need expensive tools to rank. A clean setup and well-written content go further than most realise.

Meta tags for every page

Every page gets a unique title and meta description. Open Graph and Twitter Card tags for rich link previews.

Structured data

Search engines love structured content. I add JSON-LD for business info, breadcrumbs, and FAQ sections.

Performance is a ranking factor

Target under 2s load time, under 100KB CSS, lazy-loaded images. Every millisecond counts.

Content structure matters

One H1 per page, clear heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text, readable URLs. Well-structured content is easier for users and crawlers.

SEO is not magic. It is doing the fundamentals consistently.
