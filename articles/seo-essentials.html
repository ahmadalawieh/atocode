Essential SEO for every new business website

Ahmad AlawiehJanuary 20264 min read

You don't need expensive tools to rank. A clean setup and well-written content go further than most realise. Here's exactly what I configure for every client site before launch.

Meta tags for every page

Every page gets a unique title and meta description. Open Graph and Twitter Card tags for rich link previews when shared on social media.

Structured data (JSON-LD)

Search engines love structured content. I add JSON-LD for business info, breadcrumbs, and FAQ sections. This is what powers rich results in Google.

Sitemap and robots.txt

XML sitemap submitted to Google Search Console. Clean robots.txt that doesn't accidentally block important content. Both submitted and verified before launch.

Performance is a ranking factor

Page speed directly impacts rankings. Target under 2s load time, under 100KB CSS, lazy-loaded images, and efficient font loading. Every millisecond counts.

Content structure matters

One H1 per page, clear heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text on all images, readable URLs without random IDs. Well-structured content is easier for both users and crawlers.

Google Search Console

Set up, verify ownership, submit sitemap, monitor for crawl errors. This free tool is essential and takes 5 minutes to configure. No excuse to skip it.

SEO isn't magic. It's doing the fundamentals consistently and letting quality content do the rest.
