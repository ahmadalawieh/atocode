FAQ

Common questions

Answers to what clients usually ask before we start.

How long does a typical website project take?

A standard 5-page business website takes 3-4 weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger projects with custom functionality or e-commerce typically run 6-8 weeks. I always give a clear timeline upfront during the discovery phase.

Do you work with clients worldwide?

Yes. I work remotely with clients across the Middle East, Europe, North America, and beyond. All communication happens via email, WhatsApp, and video calls.

What do I need to provide to get started?

At minimum: your business goals, a rough idea of pages you need, and any existing branding. If you have content ready, great. If not, I can guide you on what to prepare.

Can you work with my existing site or platform?

Absolutely. I regularly work on existing WordPress, WooCommerce, and static sites. Whether a redesign, new features, or maintenance -- I can pick up where your last developer left off.

What happens after the site goes live?

I offer ongoing Care plans starting at $120/month covering updates, security monitoring, backups, content edits, and priority support. Most clients stay on Care for years.

How do payments work?

For Launch projects, I invoice 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. For Care plans, billing is monthly. Payments via bank transfer or wire with clear invoices for every transaction.
